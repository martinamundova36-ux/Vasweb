The banking sector has undergone profound transformations in recent years, necessitating a continuous embrace of innovation. These changes are not merely technical; they directly impact the daily operations and well-being of employees. According to the abstract, it is essential to understand how these innovations interact with the internal state of workers to ensure not only process efficiency but also the long-term sustainability of performance within the competitive landscape of deposit money banks.

The research focuses on the specific context of banks in Mubi, Adamawa State, Nigeria. This geographic and sectoral focus provides a unique perspective on workplace dynamics in emerging economies. According to the abstract, the authors investigate whether service innovations affect performance directly or if they require a mediator in the form of job satisfaction, which is a critical question for managers aiming to optimize their human resources.

The authors analyzed how the introduction of new tools and processes within deposit money banks in Mubi influences employees' perception of their work and, subsequently, their ability to meet established goals and maintain high levels of performance.
