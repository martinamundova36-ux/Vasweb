### Introduction

The modern business landscape is undergoing a profound transformation driven by the rise of information technologies. Social media, e-commerce platforms, and cloud solutions are no longer exclusive to large corporations and have become standard components of small enterprise operations.

This trend creates a need for deeper academic inquiry to accurately measure the actual impact of digitalization on growth, efficiency, and overall competitiveness across various market sectors.

### What the study examined

According to the abstract, the study statistically examined the relationship between the extent of digital technology adoption and small business performance. Performance was measured through sales growth, customer reach, operational efficiency, and profitability.

To achieve this, the authors administered a structured survey to a sample of 250 small business owners and managers across retail, services, and manufacturing sectors, analyzing the data using IBM SPSS Statistics.

### Findings and interpretation

According to the abstract, the results indicated a statistically significant and positive relationship between digital technology adoption and overall business performance, with a correlation coefficient of r = 0.612 at p < 0.01.

Furthermore, regression analysis revealed that digital marketing adoption and financial or payment tools are the strongest predictors of performance, jointly explaining nearly 47 percent of the total variance.
