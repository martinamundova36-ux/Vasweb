# Building Customer Loyalty: Insights on Marketing and Satisfaction

In the competitive retail coffee industry, retaining customers is a strategic priority. A recent study examines how community marketing and promotions influence repeat visits.

### Introduction

The rapid expansion of the modern retail coffee industry has created intense market competition. For business owners, customer retention is now a critical strategic priority for long-term sustainability.

This editorial analyzes findings from a study published in Commercium, which evaluated consumer behavior patterns among patrons of FORE Coffee in Surabaya, Indonesia.

### What the study examined

According to the abstract, the study evaluated the indirect statistical relationships linking Community-Based Marketing (CBM) and promotional activities with repurchase intention through customer satisfaction.

The researchers employed the Stimulus-Organism-Response (S-O-R) framework to analyze how these marketing stimuli influence the internal evaluative mechanism of customer satisfaction.

### Findings and interpretation

According to the abstract, both CBM (β = 0.390) and promotional activities (β = 0.411) show positive, statistically significant direct associations with customer satisfaction.

The results indicate that promotional incentives drive repurchase intention primarily through customer satisfaction, as the direct path between promotions and repeat purchases was not statistically significant (β = 0.090).

### Methodology and limitations

According to the abstract, the authors used a quantitative cross-sectional survey design with 126 verified consumers, analyzed via Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM).

It is important to note that the study is limited to a specific demographic and location in Indonesia, meaning these results cannot be generalized to other markets or cultural contexts.
