Understanding the underlying drivers of digital success remains essential for effective operational planning.

Traditional evaluations often isolate individual skills, failing to capture the inherent complexity of modern commerce. This approach better reflects how diverse factors jointly produce business results.

### What the study examined

According to the abstract, the study by Tianjia You (2026), published under DOI 10.3389/fsufs.2026.1985183, investigated how six specific competences combine in association with marketing performance. These conditions included resource integration capability, digital marketing skills, content creation capability, customer relationship management, brand narrative capability, and platform adaptation capability.

The research also explored whether these configurations differed across varying levels of product differentiation. The core objective was identifying which competence patterns lead to high performance and how product characteristics influence these dynamics.

### Findings and interpretation

According to the abstract, data indicated that no single competence is necessary on its own for high marketing performance. The author identified three distinct configurations that are each sufficient: content-driven, platform-relationship, and platform-dependent configurations.

Regarding core conditions, findings revealed two main routes – either content and brand competences combined, or digital marketing skills paired with platform adaptation. Notably, platform adaptation capability was present across all three high-performance configurations.

### Methodology and limitations

According to the abstract, the analysis relied on survey data collected from 240 new farmers across 12 Chinese provinces. The study utilized fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA) to evaluate complex and interdependent relationships among the variables.

The author explicitly notes in the abstract that the data are cross-sectional and self-reported, meaning findings are strictly associational. The complete article text was not verified, and these limitations preclude establishing definitive causal relationships from the reported evidence.
