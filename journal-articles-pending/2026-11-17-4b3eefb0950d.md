### What the study examined

Authors Sarki Sarki, Widya Kusumaningsih, and Nurkolis Nurkolis conducted the research in Larangan District, Brebes Regency.

Organizational culture also demonstrated a significant positive effect, while both predictors simultaneously explained a substantial portion of the variance in job satisfaction.
