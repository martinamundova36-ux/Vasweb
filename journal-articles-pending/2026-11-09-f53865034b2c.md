A new study published in November 2026 by authors Noura Said Al-Jahwari, Syed Muhammad Ali Shahbaz Habib, Irfan Saleem, and Naeem Hayat in Management & Sustainability: An Arab Review addresses this exact issue under DOI 10.1108/msar-06-2026-0329.

### What the study examined

According to the abstract, the research examined how a firm's tactical green market orientation—the implementation of eco-friendly marketing tactics—influences green innovation and, in turn, results in successful green new venture creation.

The authors drew theoretically on stakeholder theory and leader-member exchange theory, while also observing the potential impact of different leadership styles within this process.

According to the abstract, the findings revealed that tactical green market orientation positively influences green innovation, and green innovation positively influences green new venture creation. Furthermore, green innovation significantly mediates the relationship between these variables.
