Research-inspired editorial · 5 November 2026

From Likes to Loyalty: Building Meaningful Digital Relationships

Scope of verification

This editorial is based on the available publication abstract. The full study has not been independently assessed; the findings do not establish causality or guarantee business results.

In the fast-paced world of digital marketing, businesses often chase metrics that may not translate into long-term customer commitment. A recent study published in the Journal of Product & Brand Management explores the complex link between social media engagement and brand loyalty.

Introduction

In the modern digital landscape, companies frequently prioritize easily measurable metrics such as likes, shares, or comments. However, it remains a critical question whether these activities truly foster long-term brand loyalty.

This editorial analyzes findings from the research by Akroush et al. (2026), which investigates the dynamics between social media engagement and loyalty within the smartphone industry.

What the study examined

According to the abstract, the study examined how social media brand engagement influences brand loyalty in the smartphone industry. The authors sought to clarify the mechanisms through which engagement with brand posts translates into enduring consumer-brand relationships.

The research paid particular attention to the mediating roles of customer engagement with social media brand posts and brand trust, aiming to understand if trust acts as the vital link between digital activity and loyalty.

Findings and interpretation

According to the abstract, the study found that social media brand engagement positively influences brand loyalty, both directly and indirectly through brand trust. Interestingly, aggregated post-level engagement was found to have only a weak effect on loyalty.

The research revealed that different engagement behaviors have varying impacts. According to the abstract, liking significantly influences trust and loyalty, whereas commenting and sharing do not. Trust emerged as the dominant relational pathway driving loyalty.

Methodology and limitations

According to the abstract, the authors collected survey data from 585 smartphone users in Jordan. Measurement validity was established through confirmatory factor analysis, and hypotheses were tested using a structural path model.

It is important to note that as we are relying on the abstract, we cannot assess the full context of the research or any potential methodological limitations that might be discussed in the full text (DOI: 10.1108/jpbm-09-2025-6314).

WEBU practical proposal

For small business owners and web designers, these findings suggest that chasing high volumes of comments or shares may not be the most effective path to loyalty. We recommend focusing on content that builds genuine trust rather than content designed solely for surface-level interaction.

In your digital strategy, prioritize trust-building initiatives over vanity metrics. Ensure your website and social media presence communicate transparency and reliability, as these elements are more likely to foster the deep-rooted relationships that lead to customer loyalty.

Conclusion

The study highlights that not all digital interactions hold equal value. For businesses, it is crucial to understand that the path to loyalty is paved with trust, not just the accumulation of likes.

The findings suggest that management strategies should prioritize trust-building as the primary pillar of the customer relationship, aligning with the need for a strategic approach to digital marketing.
