## English version

# Human capital and innovation in SMEs

## Introduction

Innovation is not just a technology purchase. People also need opportunities to learn, exchange knowledge and work with external partners.

## What the study examined

According to their paper's title, Phuong and Pham explore human capital and open innovation in SMEs, considering servant leadership, organizational support and employee-oriented corporate responsibility.

## Findings and interpretation

Human capital includes employee skills, knowledge and experience. Open innovation also draws on knowledge outside the business. The title does not establish which measured factor is strongest or quantify an effect.

## Methodology and limitations

The full text is still needed to verify sampling, research design, measures and results. Causal effects of any particular leadership approach cannot yet be claimed.

## WEBU practical proposal

WEBU recommends a simple improvement register. Assign each idea an owner, trial date, budget and outcome. Ask both employees and customers about specific problems. This is editorial guidance, not a verified study outcome.

## Conclusion

Combining skills with clear organizational processes may help SMEs. The study's precise conclusions still require source verification.
