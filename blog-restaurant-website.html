Restaurant Website: Menus and Reservations

Guests look for menus, opening hours, directions and bookings. Make these details visible immediately on mobile.

Why it matters

Guests look for menus, opening hours, directions and bookings. Make these details visible immediately on mobile.

What to do first

Update menus when prices change. A booking system should clearly distinguish an enquiry from a confirmed reservation.

A practical process

First define the page goal and customer needs. Make focused changes, test them on phones and computers, and check every link. Each improvement should have a clear purpose rather than a purely visual effect.

How to measure results

Review real enquiries, usability and search performance. Use those findings to improve the website over time. Publishing a page does not guarantee traffic or a particular Google ranking.
