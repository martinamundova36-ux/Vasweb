Digital Marketing and Electric Motorcycles: What a New Study Reveals About Buyer Decisions

Author: Martina Mundová

Energy transition and transport electrification in Indonesia bring new dynamics to the electric motorcycle market. According to the abstract of a study published in the International Journal of Science and Environment (IJSE), consumer purchase decisions are shaped by a combination of digital communication, influencer opinions, and perceived product quality.

Introduction

Contemporary energy transition is increasingly reflected in transport electrification, where electric motorcycles represent a vital market segment. However, policy incentives and charging infrastructure alone are insufficient to guarantee mass market adoption of new technologies.

Therefore, examining the market-facing factors that directly drive customer purchasing behavior is essential. Researchers sought to understand how digital environments and product perceptions influence the final decision to purchase an electric vehicle.

What the study examined

According to the abstract, the research evaluated three key market determinants: digital marketing, influencer marketing, and product quality. The objective was to assess their relationship with actual consumer purchase decisions.

The authors administered a quantitative survey, collecting data from a sample of 85 consumers who had already purchased an electric motorcycle. This empirical approach allowed for the statistical assessment of hypothesized links among the selected variables.

Findings and interpretation

According to the abstract, all measurement items exceeded established validity thresholds, and internal consistency fell within acceptable Cronbach's alpha ranges. The overall regression model proved significant, achieving an R value of 0.703 and an R² of 0.494.

An interesting finding is that digital marketing and influencer marketing carried negative coefficients, whereas product quality carried a positive coefficient. The authors suggest that quality acts as a direct positive lever, while communication activities require strategic recalibration.

Methodology and limitations

According to the abstract, the methodology relied on a questionnaire containing indicators for four constructs, with validity and reliability assessed through standard tests. Subsequent data analysis utilized multiple linear regression, partial significance tests, and a simultaneous F-test in SPSS.

Simultaneously, the limitations of this access must be acknowledged, as secondary review of the abstract does not provide the full text details. The findings reflect a specific Indonesian market context and a sample of 85 buyers, restricting direct generalization to other geographic populations.

WEBU practical proposal

The WEBU editorial team recommends that small business owners and e-commerce managers avoid blindly investing in broad digital reach without clear added value. Instead of generic campaigns, marketing teams should focus on building authenticity and clearly presenting actual product attributes.

In terms of web design and workplace management, this means tightly integrating marketing outputs with technical product specifications and verified customer reviews. Transparent communication and a focus on quality serve as a more stable foundation for long-term customer loyalty than influencer campaigns alone.

Conclusion

The study offers valuable insights into the market dimensions of electric motorcycle adoption in Indonesia and highlights the complexity of consumer behavior. It demonstrates that green transport shifts require a thoughtful balance between product quality and customer communication.

The available findings published under DOI: 10.51601/ijse.v6i4.792 remind us that successful sustainability transitions demand not only technological innovations but also a precise understanding of what consumers truly value in the marketplace.
