A Configurational Analysis of New Farmers' Marketing Competence and Performance

Author: WEBU

Digital platforms are transforming how agricultural producers market their goods. According to the abstract of a recent study from November 2026, research investigated how various marketing competences combine to influence sales performance. This article explores those findings and connects them to actionable insights for small enterprises.

Introduction

The digital marketplace offers unprecedented opportunities alongside notable challenges for smaller producers seeking visibility. New farmers selling through online channels represent vital economic participants, yet their sales outcomes vary extensively. Understanding the underlying drivers of digital success remains essential for effective operational planning.

Traditional evaluations often isolate individual skills, failing to capture the inherent complexity of modern commerce. Contemporary academic inquiry increasingly adopts a configurational perspective, examining how multiple capabilities interact simultaneously. This approach better reflects how diverse factors jointly produce business results.

What the study examined

According to the abstract, the study by Tianjia You (2026), published under DOI 10.3389/fsufs.2026.1985183, investigated how six specific competences combine in association with marketing performance. These conditions included resource integration capability, digital marketing skills, content creation capability, customer relationship management, brand narrative capability, and platform adaptation capability.

The research also explored whether these configurations differed across varying levels of product differentiation. The core objective was identifying which competence patterns lead to high performance and how product characteristics influence these dynamics.

Findings and interpretation

According to the abstract, data indicated that no single competence is necessary on its own for high marketing performance. The author identified three distinct configurations that are each sufficient: content-driven, platform-relationship, and platform-dependent configurations.

Regarding core conditions, findings revealed two main routes – either content and brand competences combined, or digital marketing skills paired with platform adaptation. Notably, platform adaptation capability was present across all three high-performance configurations.

Methodology and limitations

According to the abstract, the analysis relied on survey data collected from 240 new farmers across 12 Chinese provinces. The study utilized fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA) to evaluate complex and interdependent relationships among the variables.

The author explicitly notes in the abstract that the data are cross-sectional and self-reported, meaning findings are strictly associational. The complete article text was not verified, and these limitations preclude establishing definitive causal relationships from the reported evidence.

WEBU practical proposal

From an editorial perspective for small businesses and web design, relying on a single isolated marketing tactic is rarely sufficient. Much like the study emphasizes platform adaptation, website owners must ensure their digital interfaces are fully optimized for seamless user experiences and modern browsing habits.

When structuring a digital strategy for web platforms, we recommend integrating compelling product storytelling with robust customer relationship management tools. Rather than focusing on isolated sales tricks, enterprises should build an interconnected ecosystem where content, brand identity, and technical platform alignment work together.

Conclusion

According to the abstract, the study offers a valuable configurational account of marketing capabilities and highlights the necessity of differentiated support for diverse product offerings. Success in digital environments appears rooted in thoughtful combinations of skills rather than any single isolated trait.

Future development for small businesses online will likely demand ongoing flexibility and the ability to bridge content creation with technical platform adaptation. These insights provide a useful foundation for conceptualizing digital transformation in competitive markets.
