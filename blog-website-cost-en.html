How Much Does a Business Website Cost?

Čítať po slovensky

Understand what affects business website pricing: scope, design, content, functionality, SEO, ongoing maintenance and third-party costs.

Why website quotes vary

The cost of a website depends on whether you need a simple business presentation or a more complex solution with booking, forms, payments, multiple languages or integrations. Page count alone is not enough to compare quotes fairly.

1. Scope and content preparation

Define the website's purpose, audience and main services before development begins. The number of distinct pages, copywriting, photography, graphics and language versions all affect the work involved.

2. Design and user experience

A template may be enough for a straightforward project. Custom design makes sense when you need a distinctive brand experience, complex navigation or specialised interactions. In either case, visitors should easily find the next step.

3. Features and integrations

Contact forms, booking calendars, online payments, multilingual content and automated emails require different levels of implementation and testing. Some third-party services may also charge recurring fees.

4. SEO and technical setup

Useful foundations include page titles, descriptions, logical URLs, a sitemap, responsive layouts and secure hosting. Ongoing analytics, a content strategy and regular article production are separate services worth discussing upfront.

5. Costs after launch

Budget for your domain, hosting, updates, backups, technical support and any paid tools. Ask what is included, who owns the domain and code, and who will maintain the website.

How to request comparable quotes

Prepare a brief describing your goals, pages, features, languages, content and preferred timeline. Compare not only the headline price but also deliverables, testing, support and future change requests. WEBU provides indicative pricing and a no-obligation enquiry option.
