Online payments: security and a clear customer journey

By WEBU · Published 2026-11-03 · Updated 2026-11-04

Slovenčina

Customers need to understand the total and next step; operators need to address actual security. These are separate requirements. This article is a practical guide grounded in primary documentation, not validation of a particular payment service.

Professional foundations

PCI Security Standards Council provides merchants with payment-data protection resources. W3C explains accessible form labels, instructions and error messages. These are security and technical materials, not one buyer-sample study or proof of improved satisfaction.

Concepts that need separating

Perceived security in a questionnaire is not a technical audit. Trust is distinct from usability, and satisfaction does not establish the absence of vulnerabilities. A padlock icon, visual design or positive review cannot replace appropriate assessment of a payment solution.

Putting it into practice — WEBU proposal

Walk through payments in the provider’s test mode. Do not use someone else’s payment data or make a real purchase merely to check the interface. This is an editorial checklist, not a result of the cited research.

  • Verify that the final total and recipient are clear.
  • Check labels, error explanations and correction paths.
  • Test confirmation and recovery after interrupted or declined payments.
  • Clarify security responsibilities with the provider; do not promise zero risk.

Research source: text read, publication date unresolved

We have now read the available PDF by Lintang and Sangka (2026). Its methods describe 378 high-school students in the main analysis in Surakarta, Indonesia, a separate pilot and regression of questionnaire ratings. This is observational evidence, not an experimental security test.

Both the publisher and Crossref give 16 November 2026 as the publication date, still in the future at this revision. Publication status remains unresolved, so the findings are not used as the confirmed scientific basis of our recommendations. The original record is retained for transparency, not as certification of ShopeePay quality.

Limits and conclusion

Assess usability by observing tasks and security through appropriate technical assessment. Neither replaces the other. A regional questionnaire study cannot establish the security of every Slovak online shop or guarantee a business effect.
