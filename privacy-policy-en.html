Privacy notice

Data controller and project status

The controller is Martina Mundová, a natural person operating the WEBU pilot project. Contact: info@webu.sk.

WEBU is a portfolio and pilot project for showcasing work, testing digital solutions and exploring non-binding interest in future services. At this stage, the website does not accept binding orders or payments.

Enquiries and legal basis

We process your name or company name, contact email, area of interest and message to respond to your enquiry and evaluate interest in future services. The intended legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to voluntary enquiries and assessing demand (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), subject to balancing against your rights. We do not use these enquiries to send unsolicited marketing messages. Providing data is voluntary, but we cannot reply without a usable contact address.

WEBU Web Scanner

When you submit a public website address, the scanner processes the URL, device type and submission time to attempt a technical audit, diagnose errors and evaluate use of the pilot tool. Google PageSpeed Insights receives the URL and device type for measurement. Web3Forms forwards an automatic notification containing the URL, device type and submission time to the controller. The submitted URL may contain personal data; please use only public addresses without private identifiers, credentials or parameters. Where personal data are involved, the intended basis is legitimate interest in providing and improving the requested pilot tool and understanding its use, subject to balancing. Where the browser permits it, the scanner also reads publicly available HTML to identify possible legal indicators. The report is generated in your browser; it is not a legal compliance certificate.

Recipients and service providers

WEBU uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Web3Forms for notifications and enquiries, ImprovMX and Google for email delivery, and Google PageSpeed Insights for technical measurement. Third-party images and embedded content may involve additional providers and technical data such as IP addresses. Provider retention arrangements, contractual roles and any transfers outside the EEA and safeguards still need to be verified for the actual configuration.

Retention

We retain enquiry and scanner notification data only while needed to handle the enquiry, evaluate the pilot, investigate errors or meet applicable legal obligations and claims. Data should be deleted or anonymised when no longer needed. Provider-specific retention settings remain subject to verification.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or, where applicable, portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests, including at any time to direct marketing. You may complain to the Slovak Data Protection Authority. Contact info@webu.sk. The scanner does not make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about individuals.

Browser storage and external content

Browser storage remembers language and the privacy notice. External images and videos may contact third parties. Optional tracking technologies require prior consent where applicable; actual network behaviour and provider settings must still be verified.
