Research-inspired editorial · 3 November 2026

Why an AI chatbot should offer different ways to answer

Conversational information seeking matters to customers and employees. A conference paper titled Role of Personality in Conversational Information Seeking raises a research question, but its title does not reveal measured effects.

Let users choose

Some people prefer concise answers while others want step-by-step explanations. Offer a choice rather than inferring personality from a few messages.

Transparency before personalization

Explain which data the chatbot uses and allow users to change response style. Do not assign psychological labels without a sound basis.

A practical business website approach

Provide clear FAQs, a direct contact option and a path to a human. Test whether real visitors understand the answers.

Research note: this editorial article is inspired by a conference paper's title. Its sample, methods and findings have not been verified.
