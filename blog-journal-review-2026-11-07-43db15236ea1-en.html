According to the abstract, the study examined business opportunities and trends in India, specifically investigating the 'silver market' (elderly consumer market aged 65 and above) and the 'young premium consumer market'.

Findings and interpretation

According to the abstract, India is currently the world's 4th largest economy in terms of Purchasing Power Parity (PPP), with expectations to reach the third position by the end of the year. Youth literacy stands at 82 percent, with the abstract also highlighting literacy among young women.

Furthermore, the abstract notes that the elderly consumer segment has established financial independence and purchasing power. The young premium segment is described as well-educated, geographically mobile, and technologically savvy, with youth buying power projected to reach $331 billion in affluent Asia and $500 billion in emerging Asia.
