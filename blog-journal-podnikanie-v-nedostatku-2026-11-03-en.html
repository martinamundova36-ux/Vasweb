Building a business with limited resources: five useful questions

Small businesses do not always need ideal conditions to get started. A qualitative study of five Moroccan SMEs examines how entrepreneurs facing resource scarcity combine what they have with creative solutions and social networks. Its findings offer inspiration, not a universally proven formula.

Start with what is available

List your existing skills, contacts, equipment and time. Test demand with a small pilot before committing to a large investment.

Relationships are not a substitute for planning

Networks can provide feedback and potential collaborators. Define responsibilities, budgets and measurable objectives for each partnership.

A practical website lesson

Begin with a clear offer, an accessible contact route and basic inquiry measurement. Expand features when actual usage justifies them.

Research note: This article draws on a qualitative study of five Moroccan SMEs (Raiss, Elmoudni and Moufdi, 2026). The research describes the use of available means (effectuation), creative recombination of resources (bricolage), and the role of sociocultural networks under uncertainty. Its small sample and specific cultural setting limit generalisability. Website recommendations are editorial applications of these ideas, not outcomes directly tested by the study.
