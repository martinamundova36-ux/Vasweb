SEO for Business Websites: How to Help Google Find Your Site

Practical SEO guide for business websites: page titles, helpful content, indexing, XML sitemaps, internal links, mobile usability and performance measurement.

What SEO means for a small business

Search engine optimisation helps search engines understand what you offer and which searches your pages can answer. Technical configuration alone cannot guarantee traffic or a specific ranking. Content quality, competition and trust also matter.

1. Give every important service a clear page

Visitors should quickly understand what you do, who you help and how to contact you. If you offer several distinct services, dedicated pages with concrete examples and answers to common questions can be more useful than one generic page.

2. Write useful page titles and descriptions

Each page needs a distinctive title that describes its subject. The meta description summarises the content and may appear in search results. Avoid stuffing keywords into either field. Explain the page naturally and accurately.

3. Structure content and link related pages

Use a clear main heading, logical subheadings and links to relevant pages on your own site. For example, an article about website pricing should link to the actual pricing and services pages.

4. Check indexing and submit a sitemap

Verify your site in Google Search Console and submit your XML sitemap. A sitemap lists canonical public URLs you want search engines to discover. Submission does not guarantee indexing or immediate visibility.

5. Prioritise mobile usability and trust

Make the website readable on phones, use HTTPS, test contact forms and provide accurate contact and legal information. Optimised images, meaningful alternative text and stable layouts help visitors.

6. Measure results and keep improving

Track impressions, clicks, search queries and the pages visitors discover. Also measure whether people send enquiries. SEO is ongoing work: update content to answer real customer questions rather than chasing arbitrary keyword counts.
