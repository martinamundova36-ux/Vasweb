Business website SEO: content, indexing and measurement

By WEBU · Published 2026-11-03 · Updated 2026-11-04

Slovenčina

A search engine needs to discover and understand a page; a visitor needs an answer. SEO combines technical accessibility with useful content. Correct configuration still cannot guarantee indexing, traffic or first place in search results.

What primary documentation establishes

Google Search Central explains crawling, indexing and search presentation. W3C explains heading structure. These are technical recommendations from a provider and a standards organisation, not an experiment estimating a universal traffic uplift.

What to configure

Give each page a purpose, an informative title and useful content. A meta description proposes a summary; Google may choose a different snippet. A canonical link identifies the preferred URL, while a sitemap presents available canonical pages rather than commanding indexing.

  • Use a clear H1 and logically nested subheadings.
  • Connect relevant pages with descriptive internal links.
  • Check HTTP status, robots rules, canonical URLs and language alternates.
  • Provide readable mobile content and appropriately sized images.

Putting it into practice — WEBU proposal

Choose an important service page and verify the journey from link to loaded content. In Search Console, distinguish technical accessibility from actual indexing. Interpret changes alongside seasonality, search demand and competition. Structured data must describe visible content accurately.

Limits and interpretation

HTTP 200, a sitemap and a passed validator do not confirm Google indexing. Valid BlogPosting markup does not guarantee a rich result either. Impressions and clicks do not establish that a visit generated a customer job.

Conclusion

Remove access barriers first and improve content that does not answer readers’ needs. Evaluate specific pages over time rather than relying on fixed-ranking promises.
